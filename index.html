Why Spraoi Sports?

Technology that still feels like your club

Spraoi means fun, play and enjoyment. That idea sits at the centre of the platform: useful technology should make club life easier without taking away from the people and community that make sport special.

  • Designed around grassroots clubs and volunteers
  • Friendly for coaches, parents and young players
  • Simple, focused tools rather than unnecessary complexity
  • Built to grow feature by feature with real club feedback
